2. Description of the Application

CBT Therapy is a local-first, privacy-focused CBT (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy) self-help app for Android. It offers guided exercises, journaling, structured mood and thought records, habit tracking, standardized mental-health screening questionnaires, a curated audio library, a curated YouTube video library, and optional AI-assisted reflection tools.

Only a qualified healthcare professional can diagnose a mental health condition.
