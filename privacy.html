Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Introduction

Reflection Studio is a local-first CBT (Cognitive Behavioural Therapy) self-help app. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it's stored, and what — deliberately — never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to send it.

Information We Collect

Reflection Studio does not collect, transmit, or have any ability to see the content you enter into the app — thought records, journal entries, mood check-ins, habits, or any other self-help content you create. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server that stores your personal entries. Everything you write stays on your device in a local database, unless you explicitly export it yourself.

Financial Data

Reflection Studio does not collect, process, or store any financial or payment information. If the app offers any paid features in the future, payment is handled entirely by the Google Play billing system, which has its own privacy practices — this app never sees your payment details.

Device Information

The app does not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or device telemetry of any kind. No analytics SDK, ad SDK, or crash-reporting SDK is included in the app.

Permissions

  • Internet / Network state — used only if you choose to use the optional AI assistance feature (see below). No other feature in the app requires internet access.
  • Notifications — used only if you turn on the optional daily reminder, and requested only at that point, never on first launch.
  • Biometric / device credential — used only if you turn on the optional App Lock feature, to unlock the app itself. Reflection Studio never receives or stores your fingerprint, face data, or PIN — that is handled entirely by your device's operating system.

Data Storage

All of your entries are stored locally on your device in a private app database. The app's data is explicitly excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup system, so your entries are not copied off-device by the operating system. Reflection Studio has no server and no cloud database of its own.

Backup & Restore

You can manually export your data to a file you choose, and import it back in later (for example, when moving to a new device). Export and import are entirely under your control — the app never does this automatically or without your action.

Third-Party Services

Reflection Studio includes optional AI-assisted tools (Thought Analysis, Fear & Experiment Planner, Journal Reflection). These are off by default and require your explicit, informed consent before first use. When you use one of these tools, only the exact text you choose to submit — and nothing else from your journal, mood history, or any other saved content — is sent to our backend, which forwards it to OpenAI, our third-party AI provider, for processing. You see exactly what will be sent and must explicitly confirm before anything is transmitted, every time. Our backend does not log or store the content of what you send. By OpenAI's own current policy, text submitted through its API is not used to train its models and is retained only briefly for abuse monitoring. You can turn AI assistance off at any time in Settings.

Security

Reflection Studio offers an optional App Lock (fingerprint/face/PIN, using your device's own security) and an option to hide app content from the Android Recents screen and screenshots. No method of local storage can be guaranteed to be invulnerable on a lost, stolen, or compromised device, and these features are not a substitute for your device's own security settings.

User Rights

You can export all of your data, or permanently delete all of it from the device, at any time from within the app. Because there is no account and no server-side copy, deleting your data from the app deletes it completely — there is nothing left for us to retain, because we never had it.

Children's Privacy

Reflection Studio is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app itself collects no personal information from any user, of any age, beyond what you choose to type into your own local entries.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what data leaves your device, the in-app consent screen for the relevant feature will require you to re-confirm before you can continue using it.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Contact page.
