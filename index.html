Reflection Studio

A calm, private, local-first space to reflect on your thoughts, emotions, habits, and personal patterns — through guided CBT-inspired exercises, journaling, structured check-ins, a curated audio and video library, and optional AI assistance.

Reflection Studio helps you practise Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) techniques at your own pace: Thought Records, mood check-ins, Evidence Examination, the CBT Reality Lab, habit tracking, a guided program, and optional PHQ-9/GAD-7 structured screenings. Everything you record stays on your device unless you choose to export it or submit specific text to an optional AI tool.

Reflection Studio is a self-help application. It is not a substitute for professional mental healthcare, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are in crisis or need urgent support, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline directly.
